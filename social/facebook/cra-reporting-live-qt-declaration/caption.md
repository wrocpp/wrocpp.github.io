# EU CRA reporting rules now apply

## Body
Since 11 September 2026, manufacturers under the EU Cyber Resilience Act report actively exploited vulnerabilities within 24 hours, then 72 hours, then a final report, through a platform ENISA runs. C++ is not singled out.

The post also reads Qt's claim that commercial Qt 6.12 LTS ships a Declaration of Conformity, which Qt says does not make a product compliant by itself. It lists what I did not read and gives no legal advice.
