# Three September WG21 papers on C++26 contracts

## Body
Three papers in the September WG21 mailing concern C++26 contracts. P4238R1 (title page dated 1 September) is by Falco, Voutilainen, Garcia Sanchez and Spicer and asks the national bodies to vote No on the DIS ballot. P4381R0 is John Spicer's account of chairing the contracts study group and why he now objects. P4272R0 is Vinnie Falco's check of whether eighteen objections were settled. The last two are addressed to EWG.

I could not confirm the ballot dates or the result as of 5 October 2026, and the post says where I looked. It takes no side on contracts.
