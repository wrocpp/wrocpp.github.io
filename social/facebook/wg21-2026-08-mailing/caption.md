# Stroustrup wants C++26 contracts pulled

## Body
The August WG21 mailing runs to 48 papers and about a dozen are one argument.

Stroustrup and four co-authors want the C++26 contracts design replaced before the final ballot, calling it an existential threat to C++. Four companion papers push the same way.

Two numbers from the papers themselves keep it in proportion. The vote to remove contracts already happened and lost, SF:9 F:8 N:3 A:19 SA:41 against removal. And the related undefined behaviour framework was approved by EWG at SF:16 F:15 N:6 A:2 SA:0, with nobody strongly against.

So this is not a collapse. It is a group that lost a vote arguing the vote was the wrong instrument, before the window closes.

The papers, the polls, and what is at stake: https://wrocpp.github.io/posts/wg21-2026-08-mailing/
