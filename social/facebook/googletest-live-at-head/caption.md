# GoogleTest tells you not to use its releases

## Body
GoogleTest 1.18.0 shipped in August. Its release notes open by saying the branch will not accept new features and recommending you build from the latest commit instead.

That paragraph is not new. It opens 1.17.0 and 1.16.0 too. The release exists, and the project would rather you did not use it.

Sixteen months separate 1.17 and 1.18. Under Notable Changes, 1.18 lists one macro simplification and many bug fixes.

Compiler Explorer shows the consequence: it offers GoogleTest 1.10.0, from 2019, or trunk. Nothing in between.

Almost nobody outside Google lives at head. vcpkg, Conan and every distro package tagged releases. The packaging ecosystem assumes a release is what you consume, and these notes decline that in their first sentence.
