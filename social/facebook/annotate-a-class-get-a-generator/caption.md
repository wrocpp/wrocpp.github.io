# Annotate a class, get a generator: what reflection can and cannot do

## Body
Annotations on members and member functions can drive a std::generator on GCC 16.2: skip a field, rename one, run annotated steps lazily. Reflection cannot turn an ordinary method into a coroutine. A wrapper around a vector-returning method built 1000 elements for 3 consumed, and the coroutine version built 7.
