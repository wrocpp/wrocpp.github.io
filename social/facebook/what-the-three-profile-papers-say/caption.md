# What Bjarne Stroustrup's three October profile papers say

## Body
Bjarne Stroustrup published three papers on C++ profiles in October. One says what a profile is, one designs an initialization profile, one an invalidation profile.

I summarised each from its PDF and listed where the papers disagree with each other and with the implementation report. The post takes no side.
