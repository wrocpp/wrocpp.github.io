# GCC will decline AI-generated contributions above the legal threshold

## Body
The GCC Steering Committee accepted its AI policy on 29 July. GCC now declines any legally significant contribution that includes or is derived from LLM-generated content.

"Legally significant" is not new terminology invented for this. It comes from the GNU maintainer guidelines, where the boundary sits at roughly fifteen lines. That tells you what the policy is about: provenance and copyright, not code quality.

The exceptions matter. Test cases are exempt. Small contributions are fine if clearly marked. And using a model for research, bug hunting, patch review or debugging stays permitted, so long as nothing generated lands in the tree. Commits carry an Assisted-by tag, and only humans may submit or sign off.

The policy is provisional, with a review no later than the start of 2027.
