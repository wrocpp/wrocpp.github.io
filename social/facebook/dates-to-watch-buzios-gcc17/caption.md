# Two dates for the pre-Buzios mailing, Buzios 16 November, and GCC 17 stage 3 the same day

## Body
Three C++ dates within a month, two of them on the same Monday. I read each on its primary page on 6 October.

The pre-Buzios WG21 mailing has two dates: Monday 19 October on the N5058 agenda, and Friday 23 October at 15:00 UTC in a WG21 news item. Aim for the earlier one. The meeting itself runs 16 to 21 November. GCC 17 enters stage 3 on 16 November, and stage 3 does not accept new functionality.

My reading, not something a page states: Buzios is the second C++29 meeting, and a feature voted in there could not reach GCC 17 after the vote.
