# At Buzios, a poll to remove contracts from C++26 failed 41 to 90

## Body
Mateusz Pusz's Buzios report has the tally Herb Sutter's did not: a poll to remove contracts from C++26 got 41 for, 90 against, 20 abstaining. Contracts stay, and the new DIS ballot takes eight weeks. Pusz also writes that P3861R1 makes optional, variant, string_view, span and tuple structural. On 28 November GCC 16.2, GCC trunk and Clang trunk still rejected them as template parameter types, and the post shows a workaround that builds today.
