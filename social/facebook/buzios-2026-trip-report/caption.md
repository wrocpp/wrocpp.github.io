# At Búzios, five motions changed C++26 and 14 more went into C++29

## Body
The ISO C++ committee met in Búzios, Brazil, until 21 November.

Per Herb Sutter's trip report, it kept contracts in C++26 and voted 111 to 9, with 8 abstaining, to re-ship the standard. The project editor lists 19 motions: five change C++26, and 14 go into C++29.

A second ballot is planned and has no date. The post lists every motion and says what is not known yet.
