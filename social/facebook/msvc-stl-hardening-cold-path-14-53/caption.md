# MSVC 14.52 is a long-term release, and the 14.53 preview fixes a hardening ticket

## Body
MSVC 14.52 is now a long-term support release, and the 14.53 preview fixes a ticket titled "MSVC fails to cold-path _MSVC_STL_HARDENING bounds checks". STL hardening is still off by default. On MSVC 19.51 a hardened read one past the end of a vector stops with 0xC0000409, where the plain build printed 0. The post has four things to do with it, and says what I could not run.
