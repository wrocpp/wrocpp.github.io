# Regenerating a Qt container library from a spec, with and without moc

## Body
A reader wrote their Qt containers down as a spec an AI agent can regenerate from. Two fresh agents built it, one with moc and one with typed signals, and both pass 17 of 17 acceptance tests.

The moc version builds with MSVC at /W4. The spec itself has a lazy row map that costs 193 microseconds per removal at 10,000 entries.
