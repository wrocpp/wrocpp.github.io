# Humble Bundle sells 21 Packt C++ ebooks for EUR 16.35 until 12 October

## Body
Humble Bundle has a Packt C++ bundle on sale until 12 October: 21 ebooks, DRM-free, and EUR 16.35 unlocks all of them. The middle tier costs EUR 2.73 less and has only seven.

It includes Fedor Pikus on design patterns, two books by Marius Bancila, and Patrice Roy and Michael Wong on memory management. Part of each purchase can go to The Global FoodBanking Network.

Where a book's description names a standard it is mostly C++20 or C++23. One book is about C and one about Rust. Packt told us about the bundle; we are not paid for this and use no affiliate link.
