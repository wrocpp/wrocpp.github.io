# CppCon 2026 is onsite only, and Stroustrup opens it

## Body
CppCon runs 12 to 18 September in Aurora, Colorado, and there is no online track this year. Talks are recorded and posted to YouTube afterwards, but nothing is streamed, so remote attendance is not an option.

That is the opposite of Meeting C++ in Berlin in November, which is hybrid for its fifth year. If attending remotely matters, that is now the deciding factor between the two.

Bjarne Stroustrup gives the Monday opening keynote. Tuesday brings a Language Designers Panel with Stroustrup, Guido van Rossum and Mads Torgersen.
