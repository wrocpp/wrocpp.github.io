# Of the 14 C++29 motions from Buzios, two already run on a compiler

## Body
Fourth post in a series on the Buzios meeting. The committee adopted 14 motions for C++29, 11 papers and 3 lists of issues. Two already run on a compiler: 0o17 is accepted by Clang, and std::clmul is in libstdc++ on GCC trunk with -std=c++2d. Loop labels run on Clang behind an internal flag. Four have nothing to run yet, and the post lists the compilers I tried.
