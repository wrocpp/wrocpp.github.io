# The escaping-ref-capture check flags 6 of 9 dangling lambdas and 3 of 5 safe ones

## Body
Episode 8 of a series on clang-tidy checks for AI-agent mistakes. I wrote 9 small cases where a lambda captures a local by reference and escapes its function. The tidy-agent plugin check flagged 6 and also 3 of 5 correct cases. Clang 23.1 warned on 1, or 4 with -Wlifetime-safety, and one case escaped every tool.
