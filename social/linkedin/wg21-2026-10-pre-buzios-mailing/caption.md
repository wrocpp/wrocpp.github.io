# The pre-Búzios mailing has 89 papers: contracts, undefined behaviour, profiles and function-body reflection

## Body
The 2026-10 WG21 mailing lists 89 documents for the Búzios meeting of 16 to 21 November. I read six of them in full or in part and know the rest by title, and the post says which is which.

Five are worth reading first. P4433R0 answers the contracts dispute after the ballot. P3100R9 groups every case of core-language undefined behaviour into ten categories. Herb Sutter's P4401R0 asks whether code may rely on behaviour that a profile defines. P4415R0 is the first report on two profiles implementations, self-reported. P4395R0 proposes reflecting function bodies, with an automatic-differentiation engine as its demo.

The rest sits in a table by group: units, concurrency, text, language and process.
