# Six Qt security advisories in five days: which versions are fixed, and what to do now

## Body
Between 19 and 23 October the Qt Company published six security advisories. Two are rated high: a denial of service in QXmlStreamReader (CVSS 4.0 score 8.7) and one in the SVG parser (7.1). Two are medium and two are low.

Every advisory names fixed releases. But on 25 October the open-source download directory for Qt 6.11 listed 6.11.2 as its newest release, so the fixes named as 6.11.3 are available as patches, not as a download. Qt 6.12.0 is out and four advisories name it as fixed.

The post lists all six with affected and fixed versions as the advisory pages give them, where the fixes are, and the mitigations the advisories state: limit untrusted XML, SVG and JSON input, and turn HTTP/2 off where you cannot control the server.
