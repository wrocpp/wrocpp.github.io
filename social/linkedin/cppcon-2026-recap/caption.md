# Four CppCon 2026 keynotes are already free to watch

## Body
CppCon 2026 closed on Friday in Aurora, Colorado. Four keynotes are already watchable, and not where you would look for them. CppCon posted each as an unlisted YouTube link from its own site, describing them as prerelease and noting they cannot be found on the channel.

Bjarne Stroustrup on profiles. Timur Doumler on how C++26 changes the way we write code. Laurie Kirk on object residency. And the Language Designers Panel, which is the first time CppCon has run a panel as a keynote, putting the designers of C++, Python and C# on one stage.

A small thing worth reporting: the panel video credits four people, and the conference page lists three. Emma Tracey is the fourth. We read the titles from YouTube metadata rather than copying the programme, which is how that turned up.

The rest of the programme is on a clock. Talks land first in Early Video Access, which is paid, and CppCon states that each video stays exclusive there for a minimum of 30 days. The free YouTube release then runs at one Main Program video per business day. The figures come from CppCon's own page, which is still worded for the 2025 edition.

So for a talk you are not paying for, the wait is thirty days before the queue starts, and the queue itself runs for months.

We have not watched these yet. This tells you they exist and where to find them.

https://wrocpp.github.io/posts/cppcon-2026-recap/
