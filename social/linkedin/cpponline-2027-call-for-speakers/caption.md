# C++Online 2027 call for speakers closes on Friday 20 November

## Body
The call for speakers for C++Online 2027 closes this Friday, 20 November 2026. The conference is online only and runs on 10 to 12 March 2027.

Talks are 30, 60 or 90 minutes, livestreamed and available on demand, and pre-recorded talks are accepted. The organisers asked their community partners to pass the reminder on, so here it is.

The deadline for workshop and training proposals is later, at the end of December. The 20 November date is for talks only.
