# EU CRA reporting rules now apply

## Body
Reporting under the EU Cyber Resilience Act has applied since 11 September 2026. Article 14 of Regulation 2024/2847 gives manufacturers 24 hours for an early warning of an actively exploited vulnerability and 72 hours for the notification. The final report is due 14 days after a corrective measure is available. Reports go through a single platform that ENISA runs.

C++ is not singled out. The Regulation covers products with digital elements in any language. The Commission page gives 11 December 2027 as the start of reporting duties for open-source software stewards under Article 24(3), and the post says where the text I read is silent on that date.

Qt's 6.12 announcement says the commercial LTS ships a Declaration of Conformity. Qt writes that the declaration does not make a product automatically compliant.

The post lists what I did not read: national guidance, the ENISA platform documents, the harmonised standards and product classification. It is not legal advice.
