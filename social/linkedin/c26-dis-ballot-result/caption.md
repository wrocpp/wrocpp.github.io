# The C++26 DIS ballot was reported as disapproved: the numbers, the sources and the next steps

## Body
The 12-week ISO ballot on C++26 concluded on 22 October. Two named people report it was disapproved: Robert Seacord on LinkedIn and Mateusz Pusz in his contracts series. The tally they give is 12 of 20 P-members in favour, where two thirds are needed, and 9 of 22 votes negative, where a quarter is allowed.

No WG21 or ISO document states the tally yet, so the post marks every number as reported. It keeps to what the papers prove: the criteria, the four routes the rules allow and the 36-month clock that started on 2 January 2025. It also lists four papers with authors and dates, and takes no side on contracts.

Búzios, 16 to 21 November, is where WG21 advises the SC22 chair.
