# CppCon 2026 is onsite only, and Stroustrup opens it

## Body
CppCon runs 12 to 18 September at the Gaylord Rockies in Aurora, Colorado, and the format has changed in a way worth knowing before you plan around it: there is no online track this year.

Talks are still recorded and published to YouTube in the months afterwards. What is gone is live remote attendance. If you are not in Colorado in September you will see the content later like everyone else, and you will miss the hallway track entirely, which regulars will tell you is the actual conference.

The hybrid experiment has been quietly unwinding across the C++ calendar for a couple of years. Notably this is the opposite of what Meeting C++ is doing in Berlin in November, which is running hybrid for its fifth year. If remote attendance matters to you, that difference is now the deciding factor between the two.

Bjarne Stroustrup gives the Monday opening keynote. The marquee session is Tuesday: a Language Designers Panel with Stroustrup alongside Guido van Rossum and Mads Torgersen, moderated by Emma Tracey. Getting the originators of C++, Python and C# to argue about language evolution on one stage does not happen often.

This is also the first CppCon where C++26 is a shipped fact rather than a forecast, which should make for a different kind of hallway conversation.
