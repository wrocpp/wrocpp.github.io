# Three September WG21 papers on C++26 contracts

## Body
The September WG21 mailing lists three papers about the C++26 contracts feature. Here is who wrote each and what each asks. The post takes no side on contracts.

P4238R1, "Returning C++26 for the Evaluation It Skipped", title page dated 1 September 2026. Authors: Vinnie Falco, Ville Voutilainen, Jose Daniel Garcia Sanchez and John Spicer. It states the position of the C++ Alliance and asks the national bodies to vote No on the C++26 DIS ballot with technical comments. It requests no committee action.

P4381R0, "Chairing SG21 and then objecting to Contracts", John Spicer, 19 September 2026, addressed to EWG. It is his account of chairing the contracts study group and why he now objects. It names no vote and asks for no action.

P4272R0, "Addressed but Unresolved", Vinnie Falco, title page dated 3 September 2026, addressed to EWG. It checks the eighteen responses in P3846R1 and says sixteen of the eighteen objections were never settled.

I could not confirm when the ISO ballot closes or how it went. As of 5 October 2026 the convener's report N5056 gives no dates, and the one date I found came from a secondary source that I could not match to a WG21 or ISO page. The post lists what I checked. If you have the dates or the result from a primary source, tell me and I will correct it.

There is also a short GCC 16.2 demo of one pre assertion under the three evaluation semantics.
