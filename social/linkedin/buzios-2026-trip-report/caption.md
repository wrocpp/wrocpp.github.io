# At Búzios, five motions changed C++26 and 14 more went into C++29

## Body
The ISO C++ meeting in Búzios ended on 21 November.

Per Herb Sutter's trip report, the committee kept contracts in C++26, adopted four papers that answer ballot comments, and voted 111 to 9, with 8 abstaining, to re-ship C++26. The project editor lists 19 motions: five change C++26, and 14 go into C++29 (7 core, 7 library).

The post lists every motion with its issue link. It puts the sources on the failed first ballot side by side without reconciling them, and it says what is still unknown: the date of the second ballot, any outcome on the undefined-behaviour framework or profiles, and the full text of one adopted paper.

Three posts follow on 23, 24 and 25 November: the always-enforced contract label, the other C++26 changes, and the C++29 papers that can run today.
