# Read the Meeting C++ program and the WG21 dates for 2027

## Body
Meeting C++ 2026 runs 26 to 28 November in Berlin. The organiser's pages of 7 October say 55 talks in 5 tracks, one of them online, with keynotes from Mateusz Pusz and Kate Gregory. For the first time the conference closes with a panel instead of a closing keynote.

WG21 meets in Sofia on 14 to 19 June 2027, per N5060. The hotel books at a discounted rate until 31 December 2026. The Budapest meeting in autumn 2027 is still marked tentative on the ISO C++ meetings list.

I did not read the talk abstracts, ticket prices or the hotel terms. I read every page on 12 October 2026.
