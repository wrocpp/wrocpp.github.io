# A pimpl with an inline buffer, and three ways to get its size right

## Body
Episode 1 of a four-part series on pimpl and type erasure in C++26.

A pimpl that keeps its Impl in a buffer inside the object saves one heap allocation per object and per copy. On GCC 16.2, unique_ptr and std::indirect pimpls took 3 allocations to construct and 3 to copy; the inline buffer took 2 and 2, the same as a plain member.

The catch is the size. The header only declares Impl, so neither sizeof nor reflection can see it there. The post compares three answers on one class:

1. A number in the header, checked in the .cpp by static_assert. The C++26 message (P2741) says "set Widget::kImplSize to 72".
2. A header generated by reflection: every Impl carries an annotation, and a generator prints its size and alignment. A stale header fails the build.
3. define_aggregate in the header. Exact, but the member list is no longer hidden.

The same file, unchanged, fails on Clang with libc++: "set Widget::kImplSize to 56". A std::string is 32 bytes in libstdc++ and 24 in libc++, so a hand-written number belongs to one library.
