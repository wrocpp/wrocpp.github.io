# QRangeModel sorts its own rows in Qt 6.12

## Body
On Compiler Explorer, Qt 6.12.0 sorts a std::vector of rows through QRangeModel::sort(). The same program built against Qt 6.11.0 leaves the order unchanged.

The post reports what the Qt pages say about the Qt 6.12 LTS: QRangeModel sorting and match(), Canvas Painter leaving Technology Preview, QtGrpc request compression, the C++17 requirement and the 5-year maintenance period. It lists what I did not test.
