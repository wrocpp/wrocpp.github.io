# MSVC 14.52 release candidate: C++23 mode ready for production, and constexpr cmath behind an opt-in

## Body
Microsoft announced the release candidate of MSVC Build Tools 14.52 on 15 October. The post calls the C++23 mode ready for production use and the STL C++23 feature complete, after constexpr cmath and cstdlib (P0533R9). The stable release is expected in November with Visual Studio 18.12.

Compiler Explorer has no 14.52, so I ran the before. On its newest MSVC, v19.51, the switch /std:c++23 is an unknown option, and a constexpr std::sin fails with error C2131. The post lists what the announcement states and the open items from its comments, and says what I did not check: the RC itself.
