# Twelve Qt security advisories between 2 and 16 November, two rated high

## Body
The Qt Company published twelve security advisories between 2 and 16 November 2026.

Two are rated high. The FFmpeg backend of Qt Multimedia has a memory corruption scored 8.8 with CVSS 3.1, and QXmlSimpleReader can be made to allocate excessive memory, scored 8.2 with CVSS 4.0. The other ten are two medium and eight low. The 3.1 and 4.0 scores are not comparable, so the 8.8 does not outrank the 8.2.

The fixed releases are the harder part. On 17 November the open-source 6.12 directory listed 6.12.0 only, and six advisories name 6.12.1 as fixed. The 6.8 archive listed nothing newer than 6.8.5, while eleven advisories name 6.8.9 or 6.8.10. Qt 6.11.3 is downloadable, dated 26 October, and nine advisories name it. I did not unpack it to see whether it holds the November fixes.

The post has one table with all twelve: component, effect, CVSS version, affected and fixed versions, and the mitigation each page gives. Where a page contradicts itself on versions, it says so.
