# Mailing deadline 23 October, Buzios 16 November, and GCC 17 stage 3 the same day

## Body
Three dates fall within a month of each other, and two of them land on the same Monday. I read each one on its primary page on 6 October, so check them again if you are reading this later.

23 October, 15:00 UTC: the deadline for the 2026-10 pre-Buzios WG21 mailing, from a news item dated 22 September on the WG21 home page. If you are writing a paper for the November meeting, this is the cut-off for the mailing the committee reads beforehand. The page does not say what a late paper loses, and I did not read the submission rules.

16 to 21 November: the Buzios meeting, from the isocpp.org meetings list. That page does not say C++29. Herb Sutter's Brno report calls Brno the first C++29 meeting and Buzios the next full one, so calling Buzios the second C++29 meeting is my reading of the two pages. The Buzios agenda was not published on any page I found.

16 November: GCC 17 stage 3 begins, from gcc.gnu.org/develop.html. New functionality may not be introduced in stage 3, and stage 4 starts on 2027-01-18. My reading is that a feature voted into C++29 in Buzios could not land in GCC 17 after the vote, because the vote and stage 3 start together. I did not check which C++29 papers GCC already implements.

There is no demo for this one. A calendar has nothing to run, and every date comes from a page I read.
