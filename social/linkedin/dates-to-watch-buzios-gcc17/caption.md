# Two dates for the pre-Buzios mailing, Buzios 16 November, and GCC 17 stage 3 the same day

## Body
Three dates fall within a month of each other, and two of them land on the same Monday. I read each one on its primary page on 6 October, so check them again if you are reading this later.

The pre-Buzios WG21 mailing has two dates on two pages. The agenda, N5058, dated 16 September, lists Monday 19 October. A news item dated 22 September on the WG21 home page gives Friday 23 October, 15:00 UTC. Neither page mentions the other. If you are writing a paper for the November meeting, aim for the earlier date. The pages do not say what a late paper loses, and I did not read the submission rules.

16 to 21 November: the Buzios meeting, from the isocpp.org meetings list. That page does not say C++29. Herb Sutter's Brno report calls Brno the first C++29 meeting and Buzios the next full one, so calling Buzios the second C++29 meeting is my reading of the two pages. The agenda exists and is marked a draft.

16 November: GCC 17 stage 3 begins, from gcc.gnu.org/develop.html. New functionality may not be introduced in stage 3, and stage 4 starts on 2027-01-18. My reading is that a feature voted into C++29 in Buzios could not land in GCC 17 after the vote, because the vote and stage 3 start together. I did not check which C++29 papers GCC already implements.

There is no demo for this one. A calendar has nothing to run, and every date comes from a page I read.
