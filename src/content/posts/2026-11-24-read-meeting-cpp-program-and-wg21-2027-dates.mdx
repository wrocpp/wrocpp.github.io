Meeting C++ 2026 runs 26 to 28 November in Berlin with 55 talks in 5 tracks. The WG21 meeting after Istanbul is in Sofia, 14 to 19 June 2027. I read every page below on 12 October 2026.

## Meeting C++ 2026

The organiser's news item of 7 October says all program slots are filled. Four tracks are onsite in Berlin and one is online. Jens Weller writes that this is the 15th edition. The [earlier post](/posts/meetingcpp-2026-pusz/) covers the first keynote announcement.

- Mateusz Pusz gives the keynote "Why Great C++ Libraries Fail (And How to Fix That)".
- Kate Gregory gives the keynote "Brains And Belonging".
- The third keynote slot is "a panel or potentially also lightning talks", in the 7 October wording.

A news item of 1 October settles the third slot. For the first time the conference closes with a panel instead of a closing keynote. The live schedule lists "Closing Panel" at 16:00 on the last day. It does not state a time zone in the text I read.

## WG21 meetings after Istanbul

N5060, dated 22 September 2026, invites WG21 to the Grand Hotel Millennium in Sofia, Bulgaria. The meeting runs 14 to 19 June 2027, with an on-site orientation on 13 June.

N5060 gives three hotel dates. A discounted rate is guaranteed for bookings by 31 December 2026. On 1 January the hotel releases 50% of its held rooms, and on 14 April it releases the rest. N5060 also asks attendees to register with ISO, in person or virtual.

| Meeting | Dates | Wording on the page | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Istanbul | 2027-03-15 to 20 | no qualifier | ISO C++ meetings list, read 12 Oct |
| Sofia | 2027-06-14 to 19 | no qualifier | N5060, and the meetings list |
| Budapest | 2027-autumn | "(tentative)" | meetings list, N5058 |
| Kona | 2028-winter | no qualifier | meetings list, N5058 |
| Madrid | 2028-summer | "(tentative)" | meetings list, N5058 |

N5058, dated 16 September, still lists Sofia as "2027-summer: (tentative)". N5060 is newer and gives exact dates, so the agenda is out of date on that line. The list continues to 2030 with Crete, Porto and Los Angeles, each marked tentative. The [Búzios dates post](/posts/dates-to-watch-buzios-gcc17/) covers the November meeting and Istanbul.

## What this has not been tested on

- I did not read the talk abstracts, so I say nothing about talk content.
- I did not find ticket prices or the online ticket terms.
- The Meeting C++ news items mention no survey. The site has a survey page, and I did not read its results.
- I did not read the hotel terms behind the N5060 booking link.
